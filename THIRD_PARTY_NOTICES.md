## License compliance rules

When adding a dependency:

1. Record the direct dependency in its package/module manifest.
2. Confirm the upstream license and notice requirements.
3. Update this file when a new distributable dependency is introduced.
4. Preserve required copyright and NOTICE text in distributed packages.
5. Do not copy a dependency's license into the project's GPLv3 license.
6. For bundled native components, inspect the upstream project's own `LICENSES`/`licenses`/NOTICE material as well as its top-level license.
