# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive community.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and technical decisions;
- provide actionable feedback;
- respect different levels of experience;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, targeted insults, discriminatory language, threats, and deliberate disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep project spaces constructive.

## Reporting

For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers through the repository's available private contact mechanisms.
