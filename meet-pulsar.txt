MEET PULSAR
============

Pulsar is the friendly visual mascot associated with HWcontrol2.0, an open-source desktop application for PC hardware monitoring and supported hardware control.

WHO IS PULSAR?
--------------
Pulsar is a small, recognizable guide for the HWcontrol2.0 experience. The mascot is intended to make technical information, diagnostics, and system status feel more approachable without making the application look childish or less professional.

VISUAL IDENTITY
---------------
The design direction is a clean, minimal 2D four-point star with a wide silhouette. Its center features a simple "> -" symbol. Smooth gradients may be used to give the mark depth and energy while keeping the overall shape easy to recognize at different sizes.

The visual style should remain:
- Minimal and distinctive
- Professional and friendly
- Readable at small sizes
- Consistent with HWcontrol2.0's modern desktop interface
- Accessible, with sufficient contrast and without relying on color alone to communicate status

PERSONALITY AND ROLE
--------------------
Pulsar should feel calm, helpful, curious, and technically minded. It can serve as a visual guide in onboarding, help pages, diagnostics, and informational messages.

Pulsar is a mascot, not a security authority. The character must not imply that a system is safe, a threat has been detected, or a hardware operation succeeded unless the application has reliable evidence for that statement. Real status, warnings, and diagnostic results must come from the software's actual checks.

VOICE AND ACCESSIBILITY
-----------------------
Offline text-to-speech using Piper has been considered as part of the broader project experience. Voice support should only be described as available in a particular build when that build actually includes and enables it. Important information must also remain available as text, and users should be able to control or disable spoken output.

RELATIONSHIP TO HWCONTROL2.0
----------------------------
HWcontrol2.0 is designed to bring available PC hardware telemetry, diagnostics, and supported controls into one desktop application. Pulsar represents the approachable, user-facing side of that goal; it does not replace the underlying monitoring engine, bridge, platform APIs, security checks, or test evidence.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
-----------------
1. Keep the silhouette simple and recognizable.
2. Use motion and gradients sparingly; do not distract from telemetry or warnings.
3. Pair mascot expressions and colors with clear text labels and accessible status indicators.
4. Never use the mascot as proof that a feature is implemented, hardware is compatible, or a security check passed.
5. Keep the character's appearance consistent across documentation, the website, and the application.

IN SHORT
--------
Pulsar is HWcontrol2.0's minimal, four-point-star mascot: a friendly visual guide that gives the project a recognizable identity while keeping technical accuracy, accessibility, and user trust first.
